## 🧩 What Can You Do With OmniStudio?

OmniStudio bundles five powerful AI capabilities into a single, easy-to-navigate interface. Here's what's waiting for you:

### 💬 1. Smart Chat Assistant
Talk to AI language models just like you would with ChatGPT, but everything happens locally. Ask questions, get explanations, draft emails, brainstorm ideas, or even write code. Your conversations are never sent to the cloud.

### 🔊 2. Text-to-Speech (TTS)
Turn any written text into natural-sounding speech. Choose from multiple voices and speeds. Great for listening to articles, creating voiceovers, or helping with reading comprehension. Your PC reads aloud in a realistic voice.

### 🎤 3. Speech Recognition (ASR)
Speak to your computer and watch it convert your words into text. Perfect for dictation, transcribing meetings, or controlling apps with voice commands. OmniStudio's ASR engine works offline with high accuracy.

### 🖼️ 4. AI Image Generation
Describe what you want to see, and OmniStudio will generate original images. Create artwork, illustrations, design mockups, or photorealistic pictures. Let your imagination run wild—it's all local.

### 🎥 5. Video Generation
Generate short video clips from text descriptions. Create animated scenes, simple presentations, or creative visual effects without uploading anything to the internet.

### 📄 6. OCR (Optical Character Recognition)
Scan photos, screenshots, or PDFs to extract editable text. OmniStudio can read text from images so you can copy, edit, or search printed material with ease.

## 🧠 Understanding the Model Marketplace

OmniStudio's **Model Marketplace** is your gateway to hundreds of AI models. Think of it like an app store for AI:

- **Browse by category** – Filter models by type (chat, image, voice, etc.)
- **One-click download** – Pick a model and click "Download." OmniStudio handles everything.
- **Disk space friendly** – Each model shows its size, so you know how much space it takes.
- **Free and open** – All models available in the marketplace are public and free to use.

### ⚡ The Three Engines Explained

OmniStudio uses three high-performance engines to run your models smoothly. Don't worry about which one to choose—OmniStudio automatically selects the best engine for the model you download. But if you're curious:

- **llama.cpp** – Lightweight and fast, ideal for laptops and smaller computers
- **vLLM** – Built for speed with larger models, great for modern desktops
- **SGLang** – Excellent for modern hardware and complex tasks

Each engine is optimized to work with your specific computer hardware, including both NVIDIA and AMD graphics cards.

## 🔧 System Requirements

To run OmniStudio smoothly, we recommend the following:

### Minimum Requirements
- **Operating System:** Windows 10 64-bit or Windows 11
- **Processor:** Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 (or better)
- **Memory (RAM):** 16 GB
- **Storage:** 10 GB free space (models vary in size)
- **Graphics:** Integrated graphics or entry-level GPU (works, but slower)

### Recommended Requirements
- **Processor:** Intel Core i7 or AMD Ryzen 7
- **Memory (RAM):** 32 GB or more
- **Storage:** 50+ GB free space for multiple models
- **Graphics:** NVIDIA GTX 1660 or better / AMD RX 5700 or better
- **Hardware acceleration:** For best results, enable GPU acceleration in Settings
